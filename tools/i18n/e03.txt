795	The PDCA cycle (Deming cycle) splits a task into four steps and improves it round by round: P Plan: set goals and methods; D Do: carry out the plan; C Check: compare results with goals and find problems; A Act: keep what works and carry unsolved problems into the next plan. Good for: exam prep, learning a skill, preparing for competitions — anything that needs repeated improvement.
796	The SMART principle turns vague wishes into actionable goals. A good goal meets five criteria: S Specific: say exactly what you'll achieve; M Measurable: use numbers to tell when it's done; A Achievable: within reach with effort; R Relevant: related to what you really want; T Time-bound: has a clear deadline. Example: change "I want to get good at English" into "Memorize 30 CET-4 words every day before Nov 30 and score 80% on weekend self-tests".
